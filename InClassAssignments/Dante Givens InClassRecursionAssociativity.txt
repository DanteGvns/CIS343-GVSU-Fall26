In class: Recursion and Associativity




unary -> ("!" | "-") unary
        | primary;

This one is left associative.

This one is right recursion.

This one cannot be rewritten as left recursion.






unary -> ("!" | "-")* primary;

This one is not recursion at all.

This one is right associative.

This one cannot be rewritten without changing the prefix/postfix.
